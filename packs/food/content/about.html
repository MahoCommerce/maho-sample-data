Our story

A market stall that grew

We started with a stall, a van and three producers who trusted us with their harvest. The van is gone. The trust is the business.

Every product carries the name of the farm or the maker. We visit each one every year, taste the new season, and decide together what goes on the shelf.

The kitchen behind the shop cooks the preserves, bakes the bread and packs the boxes. If you have a question, the people who answer are the people who made it.
