Pour a spoonful into a small glass, cup it in your hands to warm it, and breathe in. Grass, tomato leaf, artichoke: that is the fruit. Then sip with a little air. The bitterness on the tongue and the pepper in the throat are polyphenols, and they are the point.

Fresh oil is loud. It quiets over the year, which is why we ship the new harvest in November and tell you to use it, not save it.
