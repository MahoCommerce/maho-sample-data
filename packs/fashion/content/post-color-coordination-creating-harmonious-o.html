Understanding color theory can transform your wardrobe and help you create outfits that are visually appealing and cohesive. Good color coordination is the secret to looking polished and put-together.

Basic Color Theory
Start with the color wheel. Complementary colors (opposite on the wheel) create dynamic contrast, while analogous colors (next to each other) create harmony. Understanding these relationships helps in outfit planning.

Working with Neutrals
Neutrals like black, white, grey, navy, and beige are wardrobe workhorses. They pair well with almost everything and provide a sophisticated base for colorful accents.

The 60-30-10 Rule
This interior design principle works for fashion too. Use one dominant color for 60% of your outfit, a secondary color for 30%, and a pop of accent color for 10%.

Seasonal Color Considerations
Different seasons call for different color palettes. Light, bright colors work well in spring and summer, while rich, deep tones are perfect for fall and winter.

Personal Color Analysis
Understanding which colors complement your skin tone, hair, and eyes helps you choose flattering shades. Warm undertones suit earth tones, while cool undertones work with jewel tones.

Pattern Mixing
When mixing patterns, ensure they share at least one color. Start with patterns of different scales - pair large florals with small stripes, for example.
