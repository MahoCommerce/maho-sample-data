As temperatures begin to drop, mastering the art of layering becomes essential. Autumn fashion is all about creating depth, texture, and visual interest while staying warm and comfortable.

The Foundation Layer
Start with a good base - think fitted turtlenecks, basic tees, or silk blouses. These pieces should fit close to the body and serve as the foundation for your layered look.

The Middle Layer
This is where you add warmth and style. Cardigans, sweaters, vests, and lightweight jackets fall into this category. Play with different textures and colors to create visual interest.

The Outer Layer
Your outer layer should be both functional and stylish. Trench coats, wool coats, and statement jackets not only keep you warm but also tie your entire look together.

Mixing Textures
Don't be afraid to mix different textures - combine smooth silk with chunky knits, or pair leather with soft cashmere. This creates a rich, luxurious look that's quintessentially autumn.

Color Coordination
Stick to a cohesive color palette to ensure your layers work harmoniously together. Earth tones, jewel tones, and classic neutrals are perfect for fall.

Proportions Matter
Balance fitted pieces with loose ones. If you're wearing a bulky sweater, pair it with slim-fit pants. If you choose a flowy cardigan, wear it over a fitted top.
