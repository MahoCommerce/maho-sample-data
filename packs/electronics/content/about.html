Our story

From a workbench

Voltline began as a repair bench in a shared workshop. Customers asked what we would buy. We told them, then we started selling it.

Every product on the shelf is measured on our bench. Headphones get a frequency response. Chargers get a thermal test. Laptops get a week of real work.

The bench is still here. If a product fails our tests, it does not go on the shelf. If it fails yours, we take it back.
