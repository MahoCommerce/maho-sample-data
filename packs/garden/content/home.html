Our story

A nursery, not a warehouse

Greenhaven is a working nursery with a glasshouse, three polytunnels and a seed bed. What we sell, we grow, and the rest comes from growers we visit.

Every plant page says which light it wants and what it does in winter. If it dies in thirty days, we replace it.
