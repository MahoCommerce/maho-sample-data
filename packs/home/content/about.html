Our story

Fourteen workshops, one shop

Hearthstone began as a search for a table that would last. We found a workshop, then another, and opened a shop to sell what they made.

Today there are fourteen workshops. We visit every one each year, and every piece carries the name of the place it was made.

Everything is built to be repaired. Oiled wood, zipped covers, joints that come apart. Ten years of parts and labour.
