Demo store

This is a demonstration store. It exists to show what Maho can do, not to sell anything, and this page is a placeholder rather than a legal document.

What this demo does with your data

Nothing that lasts. The store runs on a test installation that is reset from time to time. Anything you type into a form here, an account, an address, a review, a newsletter sign up, is demo data. It is not read by a person, not shared with anyone, and it disappears with the next reset.

Cookies

The store sets the session and preference cookies that every Maho installation needs to keep a cart, a login or a store choice between two pages. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking cookie on this demo.

Orders and payments

No order placed here is processed, shipped or charged. Do not enter a real card number anywhere on this site.

Your own store

When you run Maho for real, replace this page with a privacy policy that describes what your store collects and why. The page lives under CMS pages in the admin, and the cookie notice below it comes from the cookie restriction setting.
